# pages_drafts

The public, read-only pages: the owner's drafts (`DraftLive.Index`, `.Show`,
`.Changes`), the linked pairs (`LinkedLive.Index`, `.Show`), the published
readings (`ReadingLive.Index`, `.Story`, `.Step`) and `GET /sitemap.xml`
(`SitemapController`) of the Phoenix app. Nobody needs an account for any of
them, and none of them mints a guest.
