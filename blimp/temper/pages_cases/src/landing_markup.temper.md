# The landing page's markup

The `div#landing` element of `LandingLive`, from
`blimp/notes/web-public-extracts/landing-body.heex`. Generated by a script that
turns each `<.link navigate>` into an `<a href>`, each `<.seeit>` into its
`<p class="seeit">`, drops the HEEx comments and `phx-no-curly-interpolation`,
and cuts the four places the page depends on who is looking out into
`pagesCasesLandingDyn`. Do not edit by hand.
